My Process

How I work —
the full story

01

Discover & Research

I begin every project by deeply understanding the problem space. User interviews, competitive analysis, heuristic evaluations — building a foundation of insight before a single wireframe is sketched.

User ResearchCompetitive AnalysisStakeholder Interviews
02

Define & Synthesize

Translating raw research into clear problem statements. Creating personas, journey maps, and information architecture that sets a clear north star for the entire design process.

PersonasJourney MappingIA
03

Design & Prototype

Rapid ideation to hi-fidelity prototypes. I work in Figma with component systems that scale, creating interactive prototypes that feel like the real product — because testing fidelity matters.

WireframesHi-fi DesignFigma Prototypes
04

Test & Iterate

Usability testing with real users, A/B testing, and data-driven iteration. Every decision is validated, not assumed. The best design is the one users never have to think about.

Usability TestingA/B TestingAnalytics
05

Build & Launch

I bridge the design-dev gap by building it myself when needed — pixel-perfect, performant, and accessible. Smooth handoff documentation when working with engineering teams.

React / Next.jsDev HandoffLaunch
